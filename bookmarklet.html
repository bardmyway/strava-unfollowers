Strava Unfollowers

See who doesn't follow you back — and who you don't follow back.

💻 Works on computers only (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) — not on phones or tablets.
Method 1 · Recommended

The Bookmark Button

Set it up once, then it's one click whenever you want to use it.

1

Make your bookmarks bar visible

The bookmarks bar is the row of saved-page shortcuts just under your browser's address bar.

If you don't see it, press Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac, or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows.

2

Drag this orange button up onto that bar

Press and hold the button, move it onto your bookmarks bar, and let go. It becomes a new bookmark.

🏃 Strava Unfollowers

⬆️ Drag me to your bookmarks bar — don't click me here.

That button is for dragging, not clicking. Drag it up to your bookmarks bar.

Can't drag the button? Add it by hand instead

1. Right-click your bookmarks bar and choose "Add page…" (or "Add bookmark…"). Name it Strava Unfollowers.

2. Copy the code below and paste it into the URL (address) field, then save.

3

Go to Strava and log in

Open strava.com in the same browser and sign in to your account.

4

Click your new "Strava Unfollowers" bookmark

While you're on the Strava page, click the bookmark you just created. The tool opens right on top of the page.

Method 2

Copy & Paste (no bookmark needed)

Takes about a minute. Nothing gets installed or saved.

1

Copy the code

Click the button below — it copies the whole tool to your clipboard.

2

Go to Strava and log in

Open strava.com and sign in to your account.

3

Open the browser console

The console is a hidden text box built into every browser where you can run small programs like this one.

Press F12, or Cmd+Option+J on a Mac, or Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows. A panel opens at the side or bottom of the page.

Using Safari? Use Method 1 instead — Safari hides its console unless you turn on a developer setting.

4

Paste and press Enter

Click inside the console, paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V), and press Enter. The tool opens on top of the page.

⚠️ If Chrome blocks your paste: the first time, Chrome may show a warning and refuse to paste. That's normal. Type the words allow pasting into the console, press Enter, then paste again — it will work.

🔒 Is this safe?

  • Runs entirely in your own browser, on your own Strava page. Nothing is installed.
  • Never asks for your password — you're already logged in to Strava yourself.
  • Sends nothing to any server. No tracking, no data collection, no account access.
  • The code is fully open for inspection: browse the GitHub repository or read the full source code.
